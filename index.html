Ways to Get Involved at the New Brunswick Bike Exchange

The New Brunswick Bike Exchange is a volunteer-driven nonprofit dedicated to increasing access to bicycles and bicycle education. Through bike donations, community partnerships, and hands-on volunteering, we help refurbish bikes and get them back into the community where they’re needed most.

Opportunities include, but are not limted to:

Hosting a Bike Drive

Volunteering in Our Shop

Community Outreach

Hosting a Bike Drive: Organize a bike drive at your school, workplace, or organization to collect donated bicycles.

Volunteering in Our Shop: Support bike repairs, sales, or operations—no mechanical experience required.

Community Outreach: Work with us to provide bikes and education to local programs and organizations.

Our Community Partners

PRAB is a comprehensive human services organization that offers early childhood, youth, family, and community services to over 25,000 individuals and families annually in New Brunswick, Middlesex County, and Central New Jersey.

Girls in Gear is a non-profit organization that teaches life skills to girls and women (ages 5+) through the empowering activity of biking, focusing on building confidence, self-reliance, and leadership in a supportive, inclusive environment.


Keep Middlesex Moving (KMM) is a non-profit Transportation Management Association (TMA) for Middlesex County, NJ, focused on improving mobility, air quality, and safety by implementing programs for commuters, businesses, and residents to reduce traffic, promote public transit, walking, cycling, and ridesharing.

Mobile Family Success Center seeks to strengthen individual and family functioning and empower people to acquire the knowledge, skills and resources they need to succeed and to provide an optimal environment for children's development throughout Middlesex County.

  • How the New Brunswick Bike Exchange Works

    The New Brunswick Bike Exchange accepts donated bikes, as well as bike parts, accessories, tools, and helmets. Our dedicated volunteers refurbish the bikes, which are then sold at an affordable price to support the community.

New Brunswick Bike Exchange

The New Brunswick Bike Exchange is a non-profit volunteer based bicycle shop selling refurbished bicycles. Proceeds are donated to PRAB, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.
